Who is The Monkey King?

The Monkey King, also known as Sun Wukong, is a fictional character from Chinese mythology and literature. He is a trickster god (similar to Loki) who was born from a stone. After being born, he went on to learn various abilities and powers through Taoist magic. Spanning from 72 transformations to immortality, he could do it all. Although only one person stood in his way.

What did Wukong do?

Wukong did a plethora of things. For starters, he ate a massive amount of peaches from the Heavenly Orchard. To compliment his immortality, he went to the underworld and crossed his name out the book of death. In addition, he also drank celestial wine meant for the gods at the grand banquet. He then went on to rebel against the gods, to which Buddha himself needed to intervene.

What was Wukong's impact?

Wukong serves as a symbol of rebellion. Showing that respect or dignity is not simply earned, and that sometimes it has to be taken. He also serves as a symbol of cleverness. With this, it shows that you have to think outside the box and not abide by the guidelines life has set for you. Lastly, he demonstrates the power of the untamed mind. With this, it opens new doors to how freedom can be obtained.
